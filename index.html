Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about celebrating with us on our big day.

What is the dress code? +

The dress code is Garden Formal / Elegant Tropical. You are asked to dress in shades of brown. Since the venue is outdoors in the garden, light and comfortable formal wear is encouraged. We recommend opting for block heels or flats for ease of walking on grass.

Can I bring a plus-one? +

Due to venue capacity and our desire for an intimate gathering of 35 guests, we can only accommodate those formally named on your invitation.

Is parking available at Daphodils in the Garden? +

Yes, designated on-site parking is available at 6 Summit Close, Morant Bay. We ask that for the ceremony you park your vehicle along the road leading up to the venue entrance. You can move your vehicles in to the designated parking area during cocktail hour. Attendants will be present to help guide you upon arrival.

What time should I arrive? +

The ceremony will begin promptly at 2:00 PM. We kindly ask guests to arrive 20–30 minutes prior to take their seats.

Are children allowed? +

While we love your little ones, our celebration is an adult-only event except for designated members of our immediate family.
